The last piece: once data lives in more than one place (Salesforce objects, crawler findings,
usage snapshots), something has to unify it before an AI can reason over "the applicant" as one
thing instead of three disconnected records. This section covers what Data Cloud actually is and
why it's the grounding layer underneath Prompt Builder and Agentforce.
