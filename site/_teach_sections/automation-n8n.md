Salesforce's AI tools are excellent reasoners but terrible browsers — they work only with data
they're handed. This section covers why an external automation tool (n8n) sits in front of
Salesforce as the thing that actually touches the open web, and how it hands off clean,
structured findings instead of raw HTML.
