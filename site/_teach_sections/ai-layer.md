This is where the "AI" in the project's AI stack actually lives. This section covers Prompt
Builder (grounded, single-shot prompts wired to record data) and Agentforce (multi-turn agents
with topics and actions) — what each is for, how they differ, and how they both depend entirely
on the data the earlier sections modeled and secured.
