What this app actually is

An invite-only onboarding back office, modeled on Stripe India's real invite flow: a person applies → an AI plus a web crawler researches and scores them → a human reviewer decides → approved businesses get provisioned and supported → the invited cohort's performance gets tracked over time (cancel vs. upsell). It's not a real product — it's a realistic-enough scenario to force every Salesforce concept a real onboarding system would actually need.

The problem it's solving (for me, as the builder)

Reading Salesforce docs teaches vocabulary. It doesn't teach the fifteen small decisions — record type vs. object, Master-Detail vs. Lookup, permission set vs. profile edit — that only show up once you're modeling something with real constraints. Building one coherent app end to end, and writing down every gotcha the moment it happened, was the fastest way I found to actually retain the platform instead of just recognizing the terms.

Impact — why write this instead of just building it quietly

A working demo proves you can follow instructions. A build log with the failures left in proves you can debug a platform you don't fully know yet — which is a much closer approximation of an actual job. Writing lesson-by-lesson also forced a habit worth keeping regardless of Salesforce: if you can't explain why something broke and how you found the fix, you probably didn't really learn it.
