`Invite_Concierge` (internal, employee-facing) would let a reviewer ask for an applicant's dossier/score summary, draft a fix-request email, or get a recommendation — with one instruction that matters most: _recommend, don't auto-decide; always show rationale; never invent facts._ The actual approve/waitlist/reject click stays a human's, on the Decision Bar widget, by design — not something the agent is ever allowed to set.

`Applicant_Assistant` (external, on the Experience Cloud site) would answer an applicant's own status and eligibility questions, grounded only on their own record.

Both agents' topics, actions, and instructions are fully specified — see the project's `agentforce.md` — but building either one is a Setup-UI step that hasn't happened yet.
