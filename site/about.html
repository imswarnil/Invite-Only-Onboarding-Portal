Why "GTM engineer"

GTM engineering sits between revenue teams and the systems that run them: modeling the funnel correctly, wiring the automation that moves a record through it, and increasingly, putting AI where it actually helps (scoring, drafting, answering) rather than where it's just impressive. This project touches all three on purpose.
