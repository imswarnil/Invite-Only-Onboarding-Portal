{% include icon.html name="target" size=14 %}How it works

Four stages, one record moving through them

Not a real product — a realistic-enough scenario to force every Salesforce concept a real onboarding system would actually need.

1

{% include icon.html name="signup" size=16 %}Apply

Someone requests an invite — no login, no account. The submission lands as an Invite Request.

2

{% include icon.html name="sparkle" size=16 %}Research & score

A crawler fetches what they published; the AI layer reads it and writes a fit score and a short brief.

3

{% include icon.html name="check" size=16 %}Decide

A human reviewer approves, waitlists, or rejects. The AI recommends — it never decides.

4

{% include icon.html name="trend" size=16 %}Provision & track

Approval auto-creates the account, then monthly usage tells you if they grew or churned.

{% include icon.html name="flow" size=14 %}Architecture

Brain vs. hands

Salesforce's AI reasons over data it is given — it never fetches the web. n8n does the fetching, hands back clean text, and the AI layer writes results onto the record.
